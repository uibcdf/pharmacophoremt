# Showcase

Explore the existing native workflows through their declared inputs and controls.
These examples illustrate pharmacophoric construction and evaluation. Their
analytical or prepared geometries do not establish biological performance.

| Explore | Recipe | What you can inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A complete prepared-ligand route | [Prepared workflow](../cookbook/prepared_workflow.md) | Query construction, explicit curation, saved formats and placed evaluation |
| Rigid recovery and prepared frames | [Prepared search](../cookbook/prepared_search_workflow.md) | Essential anchors, independent negatives, search budgets and resolved frame results |
| Consensus across distinct ligands | [Prepared consensus](../cookbook/prepared_consensus_workflow.md) | Shared support, alternative hypotheses and original participant maps |
| Explicit receptor hypotheses | [Prepared receptor workflow](../cookbook/prepared_receptor_workflow.md) | Projection alternatives, exclusions and empty/failed outcomes |
| A declared observed receptor/ligand interface | [Prepared ERα interface](../cookbook/prepared_eralpha_interface.md) | Retained observations, fixed input scope, curation and independent geometric controls |

For original data, measured outcomes and limitations, continue to the
[validation collection](../validation/index.md). The [cookbook catalog](../cookbook/index.md)
contains the smaller task-specific recipes behind these workflows.
