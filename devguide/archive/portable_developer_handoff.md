# Portable developer onboarding and machine migration

The maintainer is moving development to another machine. A fresh contributor
needs the current implemented product, ownership/decisions, environment and
evidence authorities, unfinished work and safe executable next actions without
reconstructing the conversation or treating old checkpoints as current.

The initial audit finds a 4,000-plus-line checkpoint and nearly 2,000-line
roadmap mixing live instructions with history, no focused migration entry point,
and future API/validation claims presented as implemented. In-progress BRD4
method documents also link a receipt not yet published. Preserve original
scientific facts and distinguish proposed activity receiving from execution.

## Acceptance

Provide a concise read order, maintained current checkpoint/workstream map,
decision rationale and preserved historical records. Document a fresh-machine
bootstrap, exact provider/SDK authority, resource limits, verification routes,
private/ignored material transfer and missing historical resources. Correct
implemented/planned claims and validate current local links and reporting.
Publish the authorized main checkpoint and inspect its exact hosted CI, keeping
pending source/installed/public and scientific gates explicit.

Documentation delivery does not prove the destination machine has been installed,
complete either #103 study or authorize activities, release or molecular code.
