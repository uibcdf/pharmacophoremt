# Architectural, scientific and product audit — 2026-11-04

## Scope and assessment

This document records the review discussed with the maintainer on 2026-11-04,
including the revised, ordered list of 17 improvement areas. Reviewed source:
`9ea3f30077e29226a62e3dd14f39850e64eebcf3`. It is a planning assessment based
on source inspection, current contracts, developer documentation and open issues,
not a new scientific experiment or an exhaustive correctness certification.
Maintainers LMMV and dprada own prioritization and follow-up.

The architecture is well oriented and the implemented native workflows have
substantial evidence for their declared computational contracts. Predictive
utility and generalization require further experimental evaluation. The project
is an advanced scientific MVP with bounded capabilities, rather than a generally
validated discovery platform. Consolidation is preferable to an architectural
rewrite or speculative expansion.

Three questions remain distinct: whether the implementation satisfies its
contract; whether the pharmacophoric hypothesis is chemically appropriate; and
whether it predicts a declared experimental endpoint. Passing tests addresses
parts of the first question, without establishing the other two.

The [latest source receipt](evidence/native_geometry_contract_receiving_20261104_summary.json)
retains 1093 passing actual test cases and their exact environment and limits.
Those results predate this audit; no suite was rerun for the analysis. Public
0.4.0 already has installed qualification evidence under
[#23](https://github.com/uibcdf/pharmacophoremt/issues/23). Receiving current
development changes and dependencies is separate from that historical result.

An improvement area is not automatically a confirmed defect or a requirement
to implement a new feature. Some items require an audit or experiment that may
justify retaining the current design. This document stays outside the report
queues as a cross-cutting plan; independently actionable findings use their
owning issues and the [reporting protocol](reporting_protocol.md).

## Ordered improvement list

### 1. Fix confirmed public-behavior defects

Start with [#73](https://github.com/uibcdf/pharmacophoremt/issues/73): native
`io.load()` routes silently discard unknown options. Prioritize ignored requests,
lost information and failures that could produce apparently valid results.

**Completion:** valid and invalid calls have regression controls and the
confirmed defect is resolved with evidence in its owning issue.

### 2. Define the current product precisely

Publish a capability matrix covering methods, shapes, features and formats,
distinguishing supported, partially supported and experimental behavior. An
exported class, such as Shapelet, does not establish complete scientific support.

**Completion:** users can identify supported combinations and their limitations
without inferring them from names, design aspirations or historical plans.

### 3. Consolidate the public API and its evolution

Continue [#6](https://github.com/uibcdf/pharmacophoremt/issues/6): method-specific
options, defaults, aliases and diagnostics. Define compatibility and deprecation
behavior, including historical inert VirtualScreening parameters.

**Completion:** construction, evaluation and persistence have explicit,
consistent contracts and an understandable migration path.

### 4. Audit persistence and model interchange

Review validation on reading, schema versions, backward compatibility and
refusal of incompatible input. Declare what each external format preserves or
cannot represent: weights, essential sites, directions, exclusions, provenance
and relations. This is an audit target, not a claim that every reader is defective.

**Completion:** supported round trips preserve declared scientific meaning;
unsupported or lossy conversions are explicit and tested.

### 5. Establish identity, copy and state-coherence guarantees

Continue [#60](https://github.com/uibcdf/pharmacophoremt/issues/60): document
shared references, independent variants, mutations and invalidation of stored
results when model or molecular state changes. Molecular copies belong to MolSysMT.

**Completion:** supported edits cannot silently reuse incompatible results or
produce unexpected cross-object changes.

### 6. Qualify the current workflow from an independent installation

Under [#10](https://github.com/uibcdf/pharmacophoremt/issues/10) and #23, exercise
prepared input, construction, evaluation, persistence and interpretation with
identified package versions. Keep public-release and development capabilities
distinct; existing 0.4.0 qualification is not erased or relabeled.

**Completion:** a representative current workflow runs without development
clones or temporary directories and has appropriately scoped installed evidence.

### 7. Simplify documentation and onboarding

Provide short entry points for users and developers. Separate maintained
instructions from preserved history and link both. Examples should explain
scientific choices and expected results, as well as API calls.

**Completion:** a collaborator can find current capabilities, reproduce the
main workflow and identify the next task without reconstructing project history.

### 8. Review the scientific semantics of operations

Define features, matches, essential sites, exclusions and consensus consistently.
Include multi-feature sites and shared chemical participants. Existing contracts
are a foundation; audit agreement across APIs, algorithms and formats.

**Completion:** supported combinations have explicit scientific interpretations
and controls that protect those interpretations.

### 9. Complete observation-to-hypothesis traceability

Distinguish observed contacts, recognized ligand features, consensus and receptor
projections. Retain how evidence becomes a site, weight or essential constraint.
Provenance alone does not establish scientific confidence or interaction importance.

**Completion:** a model can explain the origin and assumptions of each site and
constraint without presenting an inferred hypothesis as a direct observation.

### 10. Design the experimental cohort and evaluation protocol

Resolve endpoint, experimental conditions, duplicates, chemical series, censored
measurements and ambiguous labels. Distinguish measured inactives from compounds
without known activity. Follow [#66](https://github.com/uibcdf/pharmacophoremt/issues/66)
or justify a separately declared accessible case if its source gates remain blocked.
MolSysMT owns molecular identity/comparison; study design owns experimental eligibility.

**Completion:** a viable protocol and data eligibility rules are fixed before
using evaluation outcomes to adjust the model, with exposure and leakage tracked.

### 11. Review independent scientific controls

Identify gaps in analytical oracles, adversarial cases, invariances and reference
comparisons. External implementations are comparable only under aligned
definitions and parameters. Preserve adequate existing controls rather than
duplicating them to increase test counts.

**Completion:** important failure mechanisms have justified, independent controls
and comparisons state their equivalence assumptions and limitations.

### 12. Measure sensitivity and uncertainty during model development

Study pharmacophoric parameters and preparation variants supplied by MolSysMT.
Include conformer counts and search budgets: additional search opportunities can
alter outcomes. Keep the final evaluation set reserved; development sensitivity
analysis must not become unreported tuning on that set.

**Completion:** stable conclusions and parameter-dependent conclusions are
distinguished, with preparation/search assumptions and selection rules recorded.

### 13. Review consensus, redundancy and model complexity

Test whether overrepresented chemical series dominate consensus, whether
constraints repeatedly count the same evidence and whether weights add useful
information. Compare simpler and more complex hypotheses; frequency does not
automatically imply essentiality.

**Completion:** consensus and complexity choices have an interpretable basis and
controls against dataset redundancy and unsupported importance claims.

### 14. Evaluate scoring and ranking as scientific methods

Distinguish geometric coverage, binary acceptance and any future richer score.
The current seeded_hit criterion produces ties: early-retrieval metrics must
account for tie handling without assigning scientific meaning to input order.
Measure the impact of failed calculations and incomplete searches.

**Completion:** score interpretation, tie treatment, denominators and failure
accounting are explicit; ranking claims match the information the method provides.

### 15. Execute independent validation and delimit applicability

Compare with simple baselines, quantify uncertainty and analyze errors. Use
preplanned component comparisons to assess directions, weights and exclusions.
A favorable ERalpha result does not establish performance across other target
families; broader claims require additional evidence.

**Completion:** reproducible results establish what the method contributes,
where it fails and the domain supported by the evaluated data, even if unfavorable.

### 16. Assess resources and scalability before optimization

Measure time, memory and growth with problem size. Review batching, output
retention, interruption and recovery. Some current routes materialize inputs;
establish when that limits real use before designing a replacement.

**Completion:** representative resource measurements guide necessary changes;
optimized pharmacophoric operations preserve reference results and method limits.

### 17. Adjust maintenance and validation cost

Organize fast controls, integration and expensive scientific tests according to
the changed mechanisms. Review fragility, redundancy and dependence on special
environments. Retain sufficient evidence without repeating a full qualification
campaign for every small change.

**Completion:** contributors have a justified validation route for each change
class, with reliable checks and preserved required release/scientific gates.

## Execution order and ownership

The numbering expresses priority, not a rigid serial dependency chain. First
close #73 and delimit the current product. Then coordinate two work streams:
API/persistence/installed reproducibility, and experimental study design with
scientific semantics and controls. Resource assessment can accompany both;
optimization follows measurement. Do not wait for all architectural work before
starting the study design, or use final evaluation outcomes for development tuning.

MolSysMT owns molecular representation, preparation, inspection, transformations,
recognition and reusable general molecular calculations. PharmacophoreMT owns
pharmacophoric hypotheses, constraints, correspondence, matching, scores and their
interpretation. Missing reusable provider capabilities go to the owning repository,
including DockingMT where applicable; this audit authorizes no duplicate engine.

The addon and advanced methods remain deferred unless a concrete current
scientific need changes their priority. Existing issue gates and accepted
scientific contracts retain their authority. The immediate executable task stays
#73, followed by the bounded API work under #6.
