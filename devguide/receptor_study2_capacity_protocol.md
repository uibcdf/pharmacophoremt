## Precision planning and its limits

Plan for at least50 independent eligible groups per measured class and a
prespecified binary screen decision. Using actual SciPy1.18.1
[`binomtest(...).proportion_ci(method='wilson')`](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats._result_classes.BinomTestResult.proportion_ci.html),
exhaust all success counts at n=20/30/40/50 with95% confidence. At50 the maximum
interval half-width is0.1335548568317142, below the planning ceiling0.14.
Provider/code/native identities remain stable. No ligand activity or score enters
this calculation;50 is a rounded prospective choice, not a result-selected sample.
The choice follows existing literature/search exposure and precedes the new
capacity census; it is not a blinded source search or an unseen-holdout claim.

This precision statement assumes independent Bernoulli sampling at the declared
decision/group unit. Chemical identity alone does not prove series independence,
and repeated hypotheses/conformers/states are not additional observations. It
is not power for an AUC difference or a guarantee of generalization. Actual
series dependence and unscored/failed inputs need appropriate analysis and can
prevent the target precision. Keep original/evaluable denominators and every
failure; do not replace failures or shrink the requirement after outcomes.

## Necessary capacity screen

An eligible occurrence supplies at most one independent ligand group. A source
with fewer than100 distinct tested SIDs therefore cannot provide50 groups in
each class under any potency thresholds. Source IDs are only a conservative
upper bound: alias collapse, state/series grouping, source exposure, incompatible
chemistry and missing inputs can reduce the usable count. A total at or above100
proves neither class balance nor eligibility. Unknown metadata is not zero.

Before chasing remaining method texts, census the entire already-retained
1,780-AID BRD4 gene-index universe from #106's verified receipt. The first20
descriptions were only a bounded sample; they do not define all binding sources.
Request PubChem summary metadata for those20 first and the remaining ascending
IDs in batches of50:37 batches, no individual activities/compounds/structures.
Require exact requested AID coverage and typed nonnegative SIDCountAll for every
record. Finish the entire census before ranking; retain failed or unknown records
and never promote a partially observed prefix as a complete source search.

The previously identified genetic, thermal-shift and explicit different-domain
endpoints retain their exclusions. Remaining large sources are pending actual
human BD1 construct/endpoint/method review, not accepted experiments. List them
by descending total then ascending AID for a later bounded review. Do not pool
experiments, infer classes from upstream activity flags or select by PHMT scores.
Summary delivery may include upstream aggregate categories; retain that exposure
without treating it as the declared study's class balance.
