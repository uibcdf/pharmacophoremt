## Consequences for the first study protocol

The new study needs an explicit receptor-derived construction method, receptor/preparation eligibility and a clearly defined endpoint. Reuse analytical controls as implementation evidence and the original activity cohort as already-exposed development material where appropriate; do not relabel either as fresh independent binding validation.

Reuse #66's source-method and exposure safeguards, not its frozen query/method declaration by substitution. Any receptor-derived comparison needs a new dated declaration before the affected data/score stage, with construction/tuning separate from evaluation. No new activity pages, structures, hypotheses, parameters or scores are acquired by this review. Keep binding, signaling and geometric endpoints distinct; require justified negatives rather than unspecified records or convenient decoys.

The next work package is to audit the prepared-receptor assumptions and the current provider contracts needed for an explicitly bounded receptor study, then draft that study's construction/comparison and data-eligibility protocol. Review #66's current original-text handoff before repeating any source-discovery route. The second receptor remains unselected until data/provenance/preparation feasibility has been reviewed.

All generic molecular inspection/formats/state/identity/preparation/geometry/transforms stay with MolSysMT. An uncovered capability becomes an owning-provider issue with concrete consuming requirements, not a PHMT molecular fallback. Optional TopoMT/DockingMT operations are adopted only when the declared study actually needs them. No public release, whole-library qualification, resource benchmark or favorable performance claim follows from this review.
