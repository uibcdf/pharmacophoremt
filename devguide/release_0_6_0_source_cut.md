## Coherent scope and migration

This cut adds a bounded native-feature lifecycle to the 0.5.0 workflows:
explicit native reconstruction, standalone payloads, native-only archive v2,
writer-owned immutable generations/recovery, fresh extraction provenance, and
the prepared-library feature/query recipe. The
[accumulated scope review](release_0_6_0_scope_review.md) links the owning
contracts and original scientific/installed receipts.

It also adds maximum-cardinality symmetric comparison for the admitted positive
Point/Sphere profile, with explicit correspondence and finite-limit failure.
Existing `ordered_greedy` similarity remains the default. Further public
construction/curation/inspection operations expose explicit argument controls;
the broader #6 adoption remains partial.

The static 0.5.0 comparison finds zero removed nonprivate declarations,
13 existing signatures with only additive keyword-only controls and 61 new
nonprivate implementation declarations. Those counts are not independently
qualified public APIs or a semantic-compatibility proof. Pose-evaluation and
metric-owner bytes remain identical to 0.5.0. The full source tests and scoped
receipts provide separate behavioral evidence.

Historical v1 feature archives remain readable. Native-v2 assembly and
reconstruction require the explicit admitted profile; mixed/implicit migration
is refused. Saved source/global ordinals, quantities, state/frame/atom maps and
original provenance keep their meaning. Historical inventories require actual
evaluated-family and producer declarations; feature presence is not evidence of
an evaluated-empty family. Reading or serializing saved evidence performs no
new extraction attribution and authenticates no molecular source.

Required direct floors now include PyUnitWizard >=0.28.1 and Pandas >=2.3.3.
The latter means installations selecting older Pandas must upgrade it; existing
table/CSV behavior is preserved. #206's original exact wheel passes the same
95-case selection with each authenticated public Pandas 2.3.3 and 3.1.1.
No upper caps, implicit molecular preparation or shared-prefix update follow.
