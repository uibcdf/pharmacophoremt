## Candidate neighborhood and independent controls

The selected typed anchor is the first two original query feature IDs. The case
prototype stores every distinct source-feature pair under its unordered type pair
and floor(distance/bin_width), retaining the original occurrence ordinal.
Quantization uses exact rational squared distances and integer square roots;
it does not copy the public floating norm or final matching implementation.

For exact nonnegative distances d and e and positive width h, |d−e|≤2 r implies
|floor(d/h)−floor(e/h)|≤ceil(2 r/h). The conservative study lookup searches this
neighborhood plus one explicitly named numerical guard bin on each side. The
exact-bin-only lookup is an intentionally unsafe negative control. The exact
inequality proves containment for the ideal necessary pair condition. The
additional guard and finite agreement do not constitute a universal IEEE 754,
arbitrary-scale, arbitrary-tolerance or continuous-search recall theorem.

An independent oracle enumerates full injective typed mappings. It compares
rational squared distances a,b using a+b−t²≤0 or (a+b−t²)²≤4 ab, where t=2 r;
no bin construction or backend norm is reused. Exact noncollinearity is checked
independently. Apply it to the eight strict-interior families only; retain #177's
numerical-candidate-band control separately. The cross-bin feasible occurrence
must survive conservative retrieval but be missed by the width 1 exact-bin control.
The same-bin impossible occurrence must remain a prefilter false positive that
the reference excludes. Reflection and duplicate occurrences retain their stage
and identity meanings.
