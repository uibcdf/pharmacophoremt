molsysviewer_pharmacophoremt.render.render_pharmacophore_elements(view, pharmacophore, *, tag_prefix='pharmt-site', alpha=0.45, skip_digestion=False)[source]#

Replace this add-on’s rendered elements with native spherical sites.

Only sphere shapes are supported by this minimal integration. Reject every other shape before changing the scene, rather than approximating its scientific geometry. MolSysViewer owns sphere creation, units and deletion.
